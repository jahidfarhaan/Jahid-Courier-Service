Jahid founded the company around a simple observation: people do not send “parcels.” They send things that matter to someone.

A NOTE FROM THE TEAM

“We don't have to be the loudest courier brand. We have to be the one people trust to get it there.”

That philosophy sits behind every part of the service. The booking needs to be clear. The pickup needs to be accurate. The route needs to make sense. And when something does not go as planned, there should be a human answer.

Jahid Express is being built as a modern courier company with an old-fashioned respect for responsibility—using technology where it helps and people where they matter.
